Our Story

Discover the people and the passion behind Vanguard Pest Control.

Built on Trust & Integrity

Vanguard Pest was founded in 2010 by two entomologists who were frustrated with the industry's reliance on harsh, outdated chemicals.

They believed there had to be a better way—a way to protect homes effectively without compromising the health of the families and pets living inside them. Today, Vanguard is the region's leading eco-friendly pest control provider.

Protecting Our Community

At Vanguard, our mission extends beyond pest-free homes. We invest in the health and well-being of the entire community we serve.

City Animal Shelter

Proud sponsors of the local City Animal Shelter — we provide free monthly pest control ensuring all rescue animals live in a clean, safe, bug-free environment year-round.

Free Senior Home Program

Qualifying homeowners over 65 receive one complimentary annual pest inspection and exterior perimeter barrier treatment — because everyone deserves a safe home.

Eco-Safe Product Commitment

We have eliminated all harsh organophosphate-based pesticides from our residential product line. Every treatment is botanically derived, EPA-registered, and certified safe for children and pets.

School Education Program

Our entomologists volunteer at local elementary schools each spring to teach children about beneficial insects, sustainable pest management, and environmental responsibility.
