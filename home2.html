Our Approach to Pest Control

We don't just spray and pray. We use science.

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Vanguard relies on IPM principles. This means we focus on long-term prevention of pests through a combination of techniques such as biological control, habitat manipulation, and modification of cultural practices.

Pesticides are used only after monitoring indicates they are needed according to established guidelines, and treatments are made with the goal of removing only the target organism.
