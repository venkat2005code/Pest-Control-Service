Pest Identification Guide

Know your enemy. Learn about common pests, their dangers, and how to identify an infestation before it spreads.

Termites

Danger: High

Signs: Discarded wings near windowsills, hollow-sounding wood.

Bed Bugs

Danger: High

Signs: Rust spots on mattresses, itchy red welts on skin.

Cockroaches

Danger: Medium

Signs: Droppings resembling coffee grounds, egg casings.

Ants

Danger: Low-Med

Signs: Visible trails leading to food sources, dirt mounds.

Spiders

Danger: Low

Signs: Webs in corners, egg sacs in undisturbed areas.

Wasps & Hornets

Danger: Medium

Signs: Visible paper-like nests under eaves or in trees.

4 Steps to a Bug-Free Home

Simple, consistent habits that stop infestations before they start — no chemicals required.

Step 01

Seal Entry Points

Use silicone caulk to seal cracks around windows, doors, and utility pipe penetrations. Pay special attention to where cables or pipes enter walls — these are the #1 highway for rodents and insects.

Step 02

Eliminate Moisture

Fix leaky faucets, repair pipe drips, and run a dehumidifier in damp basements or crawl spaces. Moisture is the #1 attractor of cockroaches, silverfish, centipedes, and termites.

Step 03

Proper Food Storage

Store dry goods (flour, cereal, sugar) in airtight glass or hard plastic containers. Take trash out daily, rinse recycling before binning, and never leave pet food sitting out overnight.

Step 04

Yard & Perimeter Maintenance

Trim tree branches at least 3 ft from your roofline, clear leaf pile accumulation, and keep mulch beds away from your foundation. Overgrown yards are breeding grounds for rodents, mosquitoes, and spiders.

Bug FAQ

Why are ants coming into my clean kitchen? +

Even a microscopic drop of juice or a few crumbs can attract scout ants. Once they find a food source, they leave a pheromone trail for the rest of the colony.

Can bed bugs travel in my luggage? +

Yes, bed bugs are notorious hitchhikers. Always inspect hotel beds and keep your luggage elevated on a rack, away from the bed.

Found a bug you don't recognize?

Don't panic. Snap a clear photo and send it to our certified entomology team for a free, expert identification — usually within 2 business hours.
